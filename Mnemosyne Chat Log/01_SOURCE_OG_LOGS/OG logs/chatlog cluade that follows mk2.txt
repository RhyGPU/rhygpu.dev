## What changed
The between-turn wait (`waitForBenchmarkEvaluatorJob`) was **polling every 700 ms** to notice the evaluator had committed — that poll lag stacked up every turn, which is exactly why your manual "pause → wait → re-trigger" felt snappier (you fire the instant you see it's done; the loop waited for its next poll tick).

- **Fast path:** if nothing's in flight, it returns immediately (no wait at all).
- **Safety nets only:** a slow 3 s poll (in case an event is ever dropped), the Stop check, and the 300 s cap — none of which add latency in the normal case.
