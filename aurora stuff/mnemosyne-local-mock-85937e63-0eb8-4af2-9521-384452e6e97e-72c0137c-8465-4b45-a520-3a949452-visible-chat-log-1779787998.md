OOC: Clarify phone call vs text behavior.

## Narrator
Created: 1779787882

In this simulation, **phone calls** and **texts** function as distinct communication modes with different pacing and intimacy:

- **Phone calls** are real‑time, voice‑based interactions. They demand immediate attention and carry a sense of presence—like hearing someone’s breath, tone, and pauses. You can answer, ignore, or let it go to voicemail. Calls are useful for urgent or emotionally charged conversations, and they often feel more personal and direct.

- **Texts** are asynchronous written messages. You can read and reply at your leisure, and they leave a visible record. Texts are great for casual check‑ins, planning, or flirting with a bit of distance. Because they’re not in‑the‑moment, they can feel less intense but also more deliberate.

Both can be used to build connection, but calls tend to accelerate intimacy, while texts let you pace the interaction. The system treats them accordingly: calls trigger immediate scene shifts if answered, while texts appear as notification events you can choose to respond to later.
